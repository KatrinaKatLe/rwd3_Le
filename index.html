The Seven Deadly Sins

The Seven Deadly Sins is an anime and it is one of my favorite series. I like the storyline and how the characters all change over each season. It may be emotional with each episode, but the characters and adventure makes it better and more entertaining to watch.

This is a sneak peek into the characters, story, and world within this fantasy anime.

A look into the story

Meliodas

Meliodas is known as the captain of the Seven Deadly Sins and is strong and has a cheerful personality. He normally acts playful, but he gets serious when it comes to those he cares about, just to protect them.

Character: The Dragon's Sin of Wrath

The world

This takes place in Britannia, a world filled with magic, powers, kingdoms, and legendary warriors. There are many different locations that create the world to make it into a world full of adventure, mystery and danger.

Category: Adventure and magic

Friendships and loyalty

Each of the characters have different personalities and abilities, that make it hard, but in the end they trust each other. The loyalty they have for their friends is what helps them when fighting is an important part of the story.

Theme: Trust and teamwork

Battles and Magic

The magic and battles they have makes the series entertaining to watch. Each character is special in their own way and each time they fight it reveal more about their history, relationships and what they struggle with.

Category: Supernatural powers and action
